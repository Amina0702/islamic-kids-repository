Quiz 5

Q1

Before eating, what do Muslims say?

Answer: Bismillah

Q2

After eating, what do Muslims say?

Answer: Alhamdulillah

Q3

What do we say before sleeping?

Answer: Bismika Allahumma amutu wa ahya

Q4

What do we say when waking up?

Answer: Alhamdulillahil-ladhi ahyana ba’da ma amatana

Q5

Say a dua to seek forgiveness.

Answer: Astaghfirullah
