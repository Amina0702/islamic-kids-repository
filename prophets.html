✨ Meet the Prophets : The Guides from Allah (S.W.T) ✨

There are 25 Prophets (P.B.U.T) mentioned in the Holy Quran
Flip each card to learn about a Prophet and their important message

Adam A.S

The first human and first prophet created by Allah

Idris A.S

Known for his great wisdom and for being among the first to write with a pen.

Nuh A.S

Built the ark to save people and animals from the flood

Hud A.S

Sent to the people of 'Ad to guide them to Allah

Saleh A.S

Sent to Thamud Tribe with the miracle of the she-camel

Ibrahim A.S

The patriarch of monotheism and builder of Kaaba, titled "Friend of Allah", known for his unwavering faith

Isma'il A.S

Son of Ibrahim A.S, who helped rebuild the Kaaba and known for patience and obedience

Ishaq A.S

Son of Ibrahim A.S, known for being a righteous prophet and being an ancestor to the Israelites

Ya'qub A.S

Son of Ishaq, whose twelve sons became the leaders of the Twelve Tribes of Israel. Known for his patience.

Yusuf A.S

Son of Ya'qub, known for his beauty, patience and forgiveness and gift of interpreting dreams.

Ayyub A.S

Known for extreme patience during intense suffering and hardships

Shu’ayb A.S

Preached honesty and fairness to his people, teaching honest trade

Dhul-Kifl A.S

Known as a patient and righteous prophet

Musa A.S

Led the Israelites out of Egypt, received the Torah from Allah, the one to whom Allah spoke directly.

Harun A.S

Brother of Musa A.S, gifted with eloquence, helped guide the people

Dawud A.S

A King and prophet, received the Zabus, possessed a beautiful voice, known for his wisdom.

Sulaiman A.S

Prophet, King and son of Dawud, known for his immense wisdom and authority to command the wind and jinn

Ilyas A.S

Preached to guide his people back to Allah

Al-Yasa A.S

Continued the message of Ilyas A.S

Yunus A.S

Known for being swallowed by a large fish but saved by Allah after prayer

Zakariya A.S

The guardian of Mariyum(a.s), continued to pray for a child in old age; father of Yahya A.S

Yahya A.S

Son of Zakariya, Known for piety and honesty

Isa A.S

Born miraculously to Mariyum (a.s) and received the Injeel as a guide, performed miracles and guided people

Muhammad ❤️ S.A.W

The Final Messenger and the Last prophet, brought the final message of Islam, the Qur’an. ❤️
