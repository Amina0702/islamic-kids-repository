Let’s explore the 5 pillars of Islam - The Foundation of a Muslim Life 🕌

These are five key practices that all Muslims are obligated to fulfill throughout their life

1. Shahada
[Faith]
The declaration of faith: “There is no god but Allah, and Muhammad is His Messenger (P.B.U.H).”
2. Salah
[Prayer]
Performing the five daily prayers to stay connected with Allah.
3. Zakat
[Charity]
The act of giving a portion of a Muslim's wealth to help those in need throughout their lifetime.
4. Sawm
[Fasting]
Fasting during the holy month of Ramadan to practice self-discipline.
5. Hajj
[Pilgrimage]
The sacred pilgrimage to Makkah, required once in a lifetime of every Muslim, for those who are able.
